A New Destination
For Global Education.

Vivenns International Education City is being built to bring world-class education, skills, research, innovation and opportunity together in Amaravati.

A connected ecosystem where international universities, students, educators, researchers, industry and the wider community can learn, collaborate and create what comes next.

Why Now. Why India.

India is one of the largest and fastest-growing higher education and talent markets in the world. With millions of students seeking new pathways and increasing demand for globally relevant education, the opportunity is significant.

India is also opening new possibilities for international universities and transnational education partnerships.

Why Andhra Pradesh.

Andhra Pradesh is building for growth - with education, skills, innovation and infrastructure at the heart of its future.

Amaravati presents an opportunity to build a global education ecosystem from the ground up, with international institutions as active participants from the beginning.

What We Are Building

World-Class Education

International institutions, programmes and infrastructure designed to global standards.

Technology-Driven Learning

New models of learning supported by technology and digital access.

Skills & Careers

Education connected to industry, employability and the skills of tomorrow.

Research, Innovation & Entrepreneurship

A platform for ideas to move from learning and research into application.

Collaboration

Connecting government, academia, industry and the wider community.

Build. Connect. Innovate. Educate.

A new education destination for a new generation.
