A City Built
Around Learning.

More than a campus, Vivenns International Education City is a fully integrated education ecosystem designed to bring learning, research, skills, innovation, work and everyday life together.

The city will bring international universities, students, researchers, educators, industry and the wider community into one connected environment.

The Masterplan

Designed as a living knowledge ecosystem, the masterplan brings together academic, research, residential, social and community spaces around a connected campus spine.

At its heart is the Knowledge Spine — connecting the core of the campus with academic clusters and the wider residential and community ecosystem.

Purpose-built spaces for international universities, specialist schools, learning and collaboration.
